Why only two

Small menu, big flavour.

We're a home-based bakery, so we keep the menu small on purpose. Two recipes we've baked hundreds of times means every cake comes out the same: moist, fragrant and properly banana-y.

Each cake is baked for your date, never stocked ahead. That's why we ask for 2 days' notice.

Questions

Good to know

How far ahead should I order?

At least 2 days. Weekends fill up fast, so earlier is better.

How do I pay?

PayNow only, to 8649 5495. Scan the QR at checkout and include your order reference, then send us the screenshot on WhatsApp.

Delivery or pickup?

Islandwide delivery is $12, or free for orders from $80. Delivery runs 11am–6pm. Self-pickup from our home is free, and we'll send the address when we confirm your order.

How long does it keep?

3 days at room temperature in an airtight container, up to 7 days chilled. Warm a slice for 10 seconds before eating.

Allergens?

Contains egg, dairy and gluten. Baked in a home kitchen that may also handle nuts. No pork or lard.

Can I change my date?

Free date changes up to 48 hours before. After that the bananas are already ripening for you, so no cancellations.
